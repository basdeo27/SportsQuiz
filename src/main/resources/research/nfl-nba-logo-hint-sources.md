# NFL and NBA logo hint refresh

Refreshed 27 October 2026: 32 NFL and 30 NBA teams, with one rewritten clue
per difficulty in `data/logos/nfl.json` and `data/logos/nba.json`.

Easy clues use recognisable players, geography or achievements. Medium adds
more specific milestones; hard uses origins, historic records or unusual games.
Avoid nickname definitions, direct answer aliases, mascot trivia without evidence,
and claims that depend on current records, personnel or arena naming rights.
NFL season years are distinguished from the following calendar year's playoffs.

Only hints changed; IDs, club names, accepted answers and logo URLs are preserved.
Historical names in clues refer to the franchise's identity at the stated time.
