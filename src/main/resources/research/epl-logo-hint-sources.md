# EPL logo hint sources

Refreshed 27 October 2026. Each club has one authored clue per difficulty in
`data/logos/epl.json` and the publishing manifest `assets/logos/epl/teams.json`.
Easy clues use geography or recognisable achievements; medium and hard clues
use less familiar records and origins. Clues are paraphrased, not quotations.
Avoid club names, accepted aliases and nicknames in the clues themselves.

These sources support the historical facts; they do not define the active
league roster, which is maintained separately in the manifest.
