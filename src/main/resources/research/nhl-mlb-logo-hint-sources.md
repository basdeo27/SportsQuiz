# NHL and MLB logo hint refresh

Refreshed 27 October 2026. All 32 NHL and 30 MLB clubs have one rewritten hint
per difficulty in `data/logos/nhl.json` and `data/logos/mlb.json`.

Easy uses recognisable geography, players or achievements. Medium adds a more
specific milestone; hard uses older franchise history, records or unusual events.
Avoid direct answer aliases, dictionary definitions of nicknames and unsupported
mascot stories. Use dated achievements rather than live totals or current venues.

This refresh changes hints only. Team IDs, names, answers and logo URLs remain
unchanged, including the dataset's Oakland Athletics identity. Its clues explicitly
refer to Oakland history. Utah's clues describe its own 2024 arrival and later
branding rather than the former Arizona club. Winnipeg's Atlanta origin is a
historical clue; it does not assert how the NHL currently groups statistical records.
