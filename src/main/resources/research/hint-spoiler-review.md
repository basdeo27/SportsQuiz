# Athlete hint spoiler review

Reviewed 26 October 2026.

## Scope and result

Reviewed all 60 athletes across the three first-20 research banks, covering all ten categories and 1,540 remaining selectable ideas. The 604 selectable quote excerpts contain no target first-name or surname tokens after accent-insensitive, case-insensitive checks and editorial review. This is a direct-answer review, not a new fact check or a guarantee of difficulty.

Corrected 15 quote excerpts, excluded 10 identifying-nickname options from selection, and rewrote seven image/social briefs. Existing numbered selections were preserved. Some leaks were in the longer quotes added on the previous pass; those excerpts now contain the redaction itself.

Research headings, source URLs, named relatives, speaker/context notes and explanations remain author-only. They are deliberately useful for authoring and must not be copied as visible captions or quote attributions. Indirect rebuses, distinctive statistics and famous catchphrases remain valid when they do not print or speak a name/alias.
